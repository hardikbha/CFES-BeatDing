# Hard gate: TRAIN-only matched edits and manipulation operations

**BLOCKED_INPUTS. No bank generation or approval is authorized by this preparation task.** The code validates recorded provenance and approvals; it cannot establish causal intervention validity by itself. Human approval flags are not proof of authenticity or preserved forensic evidence.

## Required unit and coverage

For one original real TRAIN source r, record six aligned, byte-hashed assets: untouched original; resize/preprocessing-only control; editor no-change x0; independently generated age edit xa; known manipulation T(x0); matched manipulation T(xa). Repeat from the **original** for younger and older; do not obtain one direction by editing the other. x0 and xa share editor revision, seed, geometry, strength and processing settings, with only the explicit age instruction changing. Record the actual crop/resize/pad/color/encode/decode order and file formats; the resize control must reproduce those exact non-generative operations, not a convenient generic resize.

T must be a defensible, documented manipulation with the same operation code, donor, parameters and seed on x0 and xa, preserving comparable identity/content except the intended manipulated region. A generic blur, JPEG degradation, source-real swap, or unrelated real/fake pair is not automatically an authenticity intervention. Prefer an operation that does not change source identity; any donor/identity-changing operator requires a separate content-confound justification. Cached SimSwap or an editor output alone does not satisfy this gate. T changes authenticity intentionally; the age edits/no-change controls must preserve their parent authenticity and relevant evidence. Review flags for preservation refer to the age/control comparisons, not to asserting that T leaves the real label unchanged.

The fixed minimum is **320 unique source originals**, ≥40 in each of eight gender×race groups, each with both age directions: ≥640 complete quartets. Exactly two validated operation families, ≥20 distinct originals per group per family and both directions. ≥5 recorded source/donor components per group; ≤8 originals per component. A repeated frame, edit, epoch or quartet draw does not increase identity/component coverage. TRAIN inventory shows only nine recorded real Female-Black identities before held-out exclusions; feasible final coverage is **unverified**, and this floor must not be silently lowered.

**Authenticity is a substantive unresolved issue:** a Qwen age edit is itself synthetic editing. It cannot automatically retain a “real” label under a detector intended to flag any generated alteration. Before admitting a pair, define the detection task and justify why this specific nuisance transformation is label-preserving for that task while T is the target manipulation. No paired BCE is used, but invariance still makes a substantive label/evidence-preservation assumption. Human recognition of the same person is insufficient. If that distinction cannot be defended, reject the pair/operator and keep response training blocked; do not redefine authenticity after looking at scores.

Age is the first intervention under this version. Gender-presentation and skin-tone edits need separate validation and new bindings; their validity is not inherited from age edits. Race labels are not interchangeable with skin tone or an editing prompt. Apparent age/demographic estimates from a model are screening diagnostics, not human demographic ground truth.

## Provenance and review workflow

1. Freeze an independent metadata-only exclusion receipt, including all held-out contributors, original/donor identity and video/source links, and a TRAIN image-hash→connected-component map. Do not expose held-out scores/images to training selection. Check the graph across all contributors; shared donors cannot be split into nominally independent components. Unknown true-person links remain a limitation. The original Ding benchmark has disclosed contributor overlap; use eligible non-overlapping sources for the new bank. Do not change the benchmark split to conceal this overlap.
2. Preserve original labels separately from reviewed additions. Record original/source IDs, family, real/fake role, group labels and provenance/confidence/uncertainty, target direction, editor/version/weight SHA, prompt, seed, processing receipt, operator code/settings/donor hashes, every parent hash, output hash and canvas. No copies overwritten. Processing receipts must describe the actual generation pipeline.
3. Two distinct reviewers independent of the generators inspect randomized contact sheets and originals at useful resolution. Require written evidence for identity, intended age direction, unintended changes (beard, hair, pose, expression, shape, texture, light/background), reconstruction/blur artifacts, no-change baseline quality, and matched operation location/strength. Review every condition/source, not a generic “all look good” inferred from another pilot. Disagreements and uncertainty require adjudication or exclusion, with reasons retained.
4. Independently validate age/control authenticity and forensic preservation; document the known T cue and why T(x0)/T(xa) change comparable evidence. Use independent frozen detector score shifts, no-change/resize controls, identity similarity and spatial/cue-specific checks as **diagnostics**, with their existing acceptance criteria fixed. Do not relax identity thresholds or accept regenerated fake evidence solely because images look plausible. Human review can find failures but cannot prove that a generative edit preserves every forensic signal.
5. Review records must bind reviewer/evidence-file hashes to all six actual image hashes. Any asset/provenance change invalidates its approval. Reject uncertain, missing, reused-across-source, no-op, mismatched-setting or held-out-contributor quartets. Preserve rejected counts and reasons, per-group/direction/operator support, original and component coverage, and exclusion counts. Do not generate replacement evidence silently or invent approvals to reach the floor.
6. `bind-bank` hashes the actual bank/exclusion receipt and invokes the code gate. Then perform each paired arm's real-input preflight and backward dry-run, review exposure summaries, and obtain an external source/config-bound launch release. Hash validity cannot replace scientific review. No train release exists now.
