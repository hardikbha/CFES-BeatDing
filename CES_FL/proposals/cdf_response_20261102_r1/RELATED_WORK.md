# Primary-source positioning (accessed 2 November 2026)

These are author paper/code descriptions, not reproduced performance claims. No SOTA or exhaustive-priority conclusion follows from this survey.

| Prior work | Relevant established direction | Consequence for CES-FL |
|---|---|---|
| Sagawa et al., [group-DRO](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08731), [official code](https://github.com/kohpangwei/group_DRO) | Minimize worst-group supervised risk with adaptive group weights; regularization matters for worst-group generalization. | Include a standard group-DRO control. Group balancing and robust risk are not novel contributions here. |
| Ju et al., WACV2024, [DAG/DAW-FDD paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2024/html/Ju_Improving_Fairness_in_Deepfake_Detection_WACV_2024_paper.html), [authors' code](https://github.com/littlejuyan/DF_Fairness) | Demographic-agnostic/aware risk-based fairness objectives. | Fairness-aware deepfake loss weighting precedes CES-FL. CVF fetch intermittently returned403 during recheck; the linked author implementation and previously inspected paper remain the scope of this description. |
| Lin et al., CVPR2024, [PG-FDD](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Lin_Preserving_Fairness_Generalization_in_Deepfake_Detection_CVPR_2024_paper.html), [authors' code](https://github.com/Purdue-M2/Fairness-Generalization) | Separate demographic/domain-related representations from forgery evidence and improve fairness generalization. | A claim merely to retain forensic evidence while reducing demographic dependence is too broad. CVF recheck returned403; do not represent a new reproduction. |
| [FairAlign author repository](https://github.com/Mkolahdoozi/FairAlign), TMLR2025 as recorded there | Conditional embedding-distribution alignment in a kernel space, including skin-tone annotation/evaluation. | Group distribution alignment and skin fairness are prior art. Our real scalar-score CDF surrogate is an operationally motivated control, not first distributional fairness. |
| Cheng et al., [DAID, NeurIPS2025](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/4bd8c35573f0a011d84f5bf5c6c887f0-Abstract-Conference.html), [official code](https://github.com/iLearn-Lab/NeurIPS25-DAID) | Demographic rebalancing, subgroup normalization and attribute-agnostic feature alignment; explicit fairness/generalization confounders. | Match data exposure, model capacity and initialization. Improved fairness and generalization cannot be attributed to our loss without these controls. |
| Hou et al., [Rethinking Individual Fairness in Deepfake Detection, MM2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.14326v2), [official code](https://github.com/Purdue-M2/Individual-Fairness-Deepfake-Detection) | Anchor learning and semantic-agnostic forensic representations for individual fairness. | Manipulation-aware individual fairness is not an untouched area. Match-operation response is a specific new hypothesis requiring incremental evidence. |
| [FairForensics, August2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.01661v1) | Expression and demographic modeling with vision-language representations and demographic balancing. | Balanced demographic training and age-aware scope are not novel by themselves. This paper/dataset is distinct from our FairForge-2 public dataset; its conclusions do not validate our labels or edits. |
| Brown/Russell, [Face-Feature Tuning, June2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.09881v1) | Post-hoc logit remapping from frozen face embeddings; group-aware and discovered-group variants. | Include/calibrate deployment assumptions explicitly. A global threshold and an embedding/group-conditioned calibrator offer different access/capacity regimes. |
| Ezeakunne et al., [FYC, 27October2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37882v1) | Real-only FPR-disparity weight perturbation followed by per-group Youden-J thresholds. | CES-FL cannot claim the first FPR-aware objective. Its single global deployment threshold and controlled matched-operation increment are narrower distinctions. Authors' reported gains were not independently replicated here. |
| Hardt et al., [Equality of Opportunity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.02413); Pleiss et al., [On Fairness and Calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.02012) | Equalized-error postprocessing and limitations of simultaneously satisfying fairness/calibration criteria. | State whether group labels are required at deployment; one common threshold is not equivalent to per-group postprocessing. Do not claim invariance guarantees equal FPR/FNR/calibration. |

The defensible target contribution is **validated matched demographic×manipulation response constraints**, with independently reviewed processing controls, one global validation-calibrated threshold, and an incremental multi-seed comparison against the same score/CDF baseline and a differentiable Ding adaptation. The architecture, frozen/finetuned backbone, signed contribution maps, rebalancing, CDF alignment and Qwen editing alone do not establish novelty. A null or harmful response-versus-score result would fail that proposed empirical contribution. Generalization needs independent held-out sources/manipulation families; a shared editing/manipulation artifact can produce an apparent gain without transferable forensic selectivity.
