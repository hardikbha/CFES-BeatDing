RECIPE AS IMPLEMENTED (build.py)
 1 master bytes read, sha256 recorded, decoded with cv2.IMREAD_COLOR.
 2 landmarks on master: canon_v2.detect_v2 (SCRFD buffalo_l, v2 canvas), largest face. scale k = IOD_TARGET / master IOD;
   INTER_AREA if k<1, INTER_CUBIC if k>1 (cv2.resize fx=fy=k).
 3 JPEG round-trip q75 4:2:0, cv2.imencode, standard IJG tables (code identical to recrop_score.py::jpeg_rt).
 4 canonical v2: detect_v2 on the round-tripped image; face chosen = the detection closest to the scaled master landmarks
   (accepted if mean deviation < 0.25*IOD_TARGET; it was the largest face in 100% of cases); else scaled master landmarks
   (declared fallback). Then canon_v2.canonicalize_v2 -> 256 px, frozen encoder JPEG q95 4:4:4. Deterministic (no RNG).
 Output layout: /home/sachin.chaudhary/ICLR_2027/fairforge2_h/<public rel_path> (same relative layout as the release tree).
