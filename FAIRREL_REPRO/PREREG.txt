7. GRADIENT-NORM DIAGNOSTIC (Q3; code/gradlog_summary.py)
  Per logged step: ||grad(lambda_k * L_k)|| over the backbone SVD factors (GCWD and SLMA touch only these), for
  k in {CE, orth, GCWD, SLMA}; also raw (unweighted) norms, cosine with the CE gradient, GCWD per-band D_l.
  Rule (from AUDIT G3): a term is "INACTIVE" if the median over all logged steps of
  ||grad(lambda_k L_k)||_backbone / ||grad(L_CE)||_backbone < 0.01. Also reported per epoch.

8. DEMOGRAPHIC PROBE (Q4; code/probe.py; FF++ VAL only, no TEST)
  Features: (a) frozen CLIP ViT-L/14 (untrained FairReLDetector: W_p + residual = original W exactly) CLS post-LN
  z; (b) trained FAIRREL and CTRL best.pt z (the classifier input, "penultimate") for each seed. Secondary:
  z_delta (residual-only path, SLMA's input) for (b); the 1-d classifier logit for (b).
  Targets: 6-way subgroup (primary), gender (2), race (3). Frames: all val frames (primary); reals only (secondary).
  Probe: StandardScaler + multinomial LogisticRegression(C=1.0, lbfgs, max_iter 3000), fixed, no tuning.
  CV: 5-fold GroupKFold on identity components (union of the two ids in Method_a_b and youtube_a; 198 in val),
  so no identity crosses train/test folds. Out-of-fold predictions -> accuracy and balanced accuracy.
  Baselines: majority-class accuracy (training-fold majority, out-of-fold) and chance balanced accuracy 1/K.
  CI: 2,000-rep component-cluster bootstrap of the out-of-fold predictions (and of probe - baseline).
  Decision (G2a): demographic information is "PRESENT" in a feature set if the CI of (balanced acc - 1/K) has its
  lower bound > 0.05 AND the CI of (acc - majority acc) has lower bound > 0. For the trained arms, required in all
  3 seeds. If ABSENT in the trained z: Direction A's adversary/decorrelation have nothing to remove -> drop L_adv /
  L_sep from the main claim (AUDIT G2). If PRESENT: Direction A screens may proceed (subject to coordinator/PI).
  Caveat registered in advance: swap fakes carry the swapped-in identity's label (6-10% label noise).
