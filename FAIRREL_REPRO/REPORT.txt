3. GATE G1 INTERPRETATION (per PREREG section 6 debug order, partially executed — 1 seed only, so this is
   indicative, not conclusive)
  FF++ and DFDC both fail the +-2/+-3 point tolerance, in OPPOSITE directions (ours worse on FF++, ours better
  on DFDC), which argues against a single systematic bug (e.g. a uniformly wrong crop or threshold would move
  both the same way). Plausible contributors, none isolated in the single seed available:
    - Different frame population: FairReL's own published counts (FF++ 75,629; DFDC 63,225) do not match Lin's
      release used here (FF++ 116,258; DFDC 20,007/18,851) — the audit already flagged this as unresolved; we
      did not get the chance to run the per-method / train-vs-val-AUC debug checks in PREREG section 6 because
      only one seed was ever going to inform them and the queue was pulled before a second data point existed.
    - FF++'s selection noise: both arms stopped at epoch 6/10, and L_fairrel_protocol (a different crop/split)
      saw its selection score swing with the same val F_FPR instability (small F-B/M-B val cells) — the audit's
      documented risk 4 (checkpoint-selection fragility) is visibly present here too (e.g. CTRL_s7's own
      history shows score dropping from epoch 1's 21.1 to epoch 2's 11.8 before climbing back to 32.2 by
      epoch 9 — training log, logs/runs/CTRL_s7.log).
    - DFDC's higher AUC than the paper could reflect Lin's full released DFDC set (18,851 unique frames) being
      easier/more favorably distributed than whatever FairReL's own 63,225-frame preprocessing produced; this
      is exactly the "frame counts differ, exact match not expected" caveat PREREG section 6 pre-registered,
      just landing in the unexpected (better) direction for DFDC specifically.
  No conclusion is drawn beyond: the paper's absolute numbers do not reproduce from Lin's released data with
  the official code, in either direction, on a single seed — consistent with risk R3 in AUDIT_AND_PLAN.md
  ("beat FairReL may reduce to beat ERM") remaining live, but not more confirmed than it already was.
