## 1. Current conclusion and scope

We are testing whether demographic counterfactual training improves deepfake fairness beyond the effect of a detection-trained encoder. The present branch is **CES-FL v0.2.1, an exploratory, single-seed (17), Xception invariance pilot** on the archived Ding/Lin FF++ split. It is distinct from the earlier preregistered CLIP CESel-MA study. It has completed training and validation, but **has not evaluated the real held-out test sets**.

The current invariance pilot does not establish an overall fairness improvement over its matched BCE baseline. Do not claim that it beats Ding, evaluates full forensic selectivity, or establishes cross-domain generalization. Do not rename historical CFES/BD0/CLIP results as current CES-FL results.

## 3. Meaning of P, A, B, C and B/C

| Arm | Encoder history and training | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| P_pretrain | ImageNet-initialized Xception; encoder plus CES-FL heads/gates/fusion trained end-to-end with BCE on original TRAIN images; BatchNorm updates. | Produce a detection-pretrained encoder. |
| A_imagenet_frozen | Frozen ImageNet Xception and BatchNorm; fresh CES-FL heads/gates/fusion; BCE only. | Weak-encoder reference. |
| B_detection_frozen | Load selected P encoder including BN state, freeze it; fresh CES-FL heads/gates/fusion; BCE on originals. | Strong matched baseline. |
| C_detection_invariance | Exactly the same P encoder as B, identical fresh head initialization; frozen encoder/BN; BCE plus approved demographic-pair invariance. | Current proposed-treatment pilot. |

**B/C means comparing B versus C, not an additional model.** A versus B measures the effect of detection pretraining; B versus C measures the effect of the current invariance treatment. There is no separately verified current method called ARMAT in the inspected launch/audit records.
