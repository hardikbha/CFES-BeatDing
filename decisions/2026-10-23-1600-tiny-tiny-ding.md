# Tiny Tiny Ding (TTD): proportional proxy of Ding's FF++ protocol — user approved 23 Oct 2026

Purpose: fast screening of new ideas (~30-40 min/run) whose numbers predict the full-protocol numbers.
Design (fixed before any TTD run):
- TTD-train = 20% of DINGREPRO_LIN/data/ff++_ding/train.csv, sampled by WHOLE VIDEO (video id from img_path),
  stratified by label x manipulation (spe_label) x intersec_label, original proportions kept; sampling seed 2026.
- TTD-val = 20% of realval.csv + fakeval.csv, same rule. Source pairs = pairs_ffpp_train.csv restricted to TTD-train videos.
- Test sets stay FULL (FF++, DFDC, Celeb-DF, DFD). Phase 2 starts from the existing full-data phase-1 checkpoints.
- Same selection rule (Amendment 9) and calibration as the full runs, on TTD-val.
Acceptance (proxy validity), computed once the validation runs finish:
- Runs: detection-only control, A, B, C, D, E(w=3) at seed 7, plus D seeds 17 and 27, all on TTD.
- TTD is accepted if (i) Spearman rank correlation between TTD and full-data mean cross-domain AUC across the 6
  seed-7 arms >= 0.8, and (ii) |TTD AUC - full AUC| <= 2 points on average per test set. Fairness metrics are reported
  but not part of acceptance (too noisy at this scale). If not accepted: report and do not use TTD for decisions.

## Amendment 1 (23 Oct, before any TTD run; build-time diagnostics only, no results seen)
