## Failure modes (plain words)

1. **False alarms on real DFD footage are broad, and the matched threshold sits inside the real-score bulk.** 1003/3092 real
   frames (32.4%) are flagged at matched; 83/104 real videos have at least one FP and
   21 videos are needed to cover half of them. The threshold (-73.8) is only 6.5 logits
   above the real median (-80.2); per-actor real medians range -94.7 to
   -67.1. Context: Ding repro is worse on both rates (59.0% FPR at 83.6% TPR vs ours
   32.4% at 89.2%).
2. **The intersectional FPR disparity is one person in three videos.** F-Black has 3 real videos (88 frames),
   all actor 19, FPR 50.0% vs pooled 32.4%. Removing one video
   (`0_19__podium_speech_happy`, 96.7% FP) cuts intersection F_FPR from 29.05 to 18.06; removing two gives
   13.38; ten gives 0.59. Video-bootstrap 95% CI [9.3,
   81.5]: the DFD F_FPR number is not stable.
3. **Xu labels hide a gender gap.** Actor 19 is Xu-labelled M-Black in 8 videos and F-Black in 3; AI-Face cleaner (video majority) says non-male for 10 of 11.
   With Xu (metric) labels FPR is male 30.2% vs non-male 34.4%;
   with cleaner frame labels it is 22.2% vs 38.7%,
   and with FairFace video sex 23.1% vs 38.0%.
   Real women are flagged more often than real men.
4. **Outdoor walking scenes cause false alarms; static indoor scenes do not.** Real FPR 69.0%
   (walking_down_street_outside_angry) and 65.8% (walking_outside_cafe_disgusted) vs
   14.8% (kitchen_still) and 10.8% (talking_against_wall).
5. **Skin tone is not the FP driver for us (it is for Ding).** Our FPR across the declared ITA quartiles (dark to light) is
   32.8 / 36.5 /
   26.2 / 33.0%, and
   31.6-33.3% on DFD's own
   quartiles (flat); Ding's falls from 82.7% (darkest) to
   43.5% (lightest). Age: <30 34.7% vs
   30-44 24.3% (InsightFace).
6. **Misses cluster in two actor pairs and in two-person or moving scenes.** FNR by first actor: 01 (all `01<-27`) 39.9%,
   17 (`17<-08`) 33.5%, vs 0.6% for 16. By scene: walking_and_outside_surprised
   44.4%, walking_down_indoor_hall_disgust 36.7%, kitchen_still
   0.0%. Where the crop's gender matches neither named actor (likely the unswapped second person),
   FNR is 70.6% (136 frames, 10 videos) vs 10.5%; some of these are label noise in the
   test set, not detector errors. 120 real frames are byte-identical duplicates across actors 22 and 23.
7. **False alarms are mostly shared with Ding; misses are mostly ours alone.** 71.7% of our FPs are also Ding FPs
   (Ding's FPR is 59.0%, so that is barely above chance). 43.1% of our FNs are also Ding FNs
   (2.6x Ding's 16.4% base rate) but 388 FN frames are ours alone. The largest group is `17<-08`:
   `0_17_08__podium_speech_happy__ISS1V0B1` is 100% missed by us and 0% by Ding. When Ding is re-thresholded to make
   the same number of errors (test-oracle), overlap is only 39.2% (FP) and
   31.1% (FN).
8. **Some errors are very confident.** The top FP has logit 66.5 (68 FPs are at logit >= 0, 103 survive
   A1); the worst FN has logit -110.5. Moving to A1 cuts FPs to 103 (3.3%) but raises FNR to
   31.7%.
